How it works

One round a week.

Stock picking finally has a scoreboard. Compete against real people with a real portfolio, and only risk the ticket.

01

Build

Pick 3–10 tokenized stocks and funds, set the weights, type an amount. One approval swaps your USDC into exactly that basket.

02

Or back one

Like someone else's pick? Back it with a $5 ticket. Same basket, same result, and its creator earns 10% of what you win.

03

Beat the median

Entries close Monday. Through Friday, every ETF is ranked against the round's median return. Above the line wins.

04

Get paid

The program settles on-chain. Winnings and your entire basket arrive automatically. Nothing to claim.

Payouts

Half the field wins. The best win big.

Winners split the losing tickets, weighted by how close they finished to the top. IndX never plays against you: it's pari-mutuel, with a 10% take (half refills thin rounds through the season pot).

Under the hood

Fully on-chain, fully checkable.

Anchor program

Escrowed baskets, on-chain settlement, claims and voids. The program checks that the money adds up.

Autonomous keeper

Opens rounds, records prices from Pyth and Jupiter, settles and pays out. One round at a time.

Verifiable rounds

Inputs are published, their hash is stored on-chain, and every round page links its transactions on Solscan.

Tested

55 automated tests, including full rounds from open to payout on LiteSVM.
