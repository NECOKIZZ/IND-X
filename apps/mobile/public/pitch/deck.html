How it works

One round a week.

1 · Build

Pick 3–10 tokens, set weights, choose an amount. Or back an ETF you like.

2 · Lock

USDC to basket and $5 ticket in one approval. Entries close Monday.

3 · Compete

Live standings against the round's median, Monday to Friday.

4 · Get paid

Settled on-chain Friday. Winners paid, every basket returned, automatically.

Rules: no token above 50%, crypto ≤ 20%, $10 minimum basket4

Why the median

Half the field wins,
whatever the market does.

Market-neutral

The line is the field, not zero. Only relative skill counts.

~50% win weekly

vs ~10% in top-N formats. Beginners stay; skill still pays steeply.

Capital returned

Your basket comes back with its gains or losses. Only $5 is at stake.
