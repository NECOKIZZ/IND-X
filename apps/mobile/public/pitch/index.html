IndX
Pitch · October 2026

Build an ETF.
Beat the median.
Get paid.

IndX is a weekly stock-ETF league on Solana. Players build a basket of tokenized stocks and enter with a $5 ticket. Every ETF that finishes above the round's median return wins the tickets of those below it, and everyone gets their stocks back. Start with the report or the deck.
